Welcome. This is the first issue of the Eigenius newsletter — a
weekly note on the state of the project for everyone building with
it, contributing to it, or just watching it take shape.

## What this newsletter is

Eigenius is an open-source typed knowledge graph for AI-assisted
scientific reasoning: every claim records *how* it was justified, so
a brilliant analysis and a confident hallucination can no longer look
the same in prose. The project moves quickly, and the commit log is
not a friendly way to follow it. This newsletter is.

Each week you can expect:

- **What landed** — the changes that matter, in plain language.
- **What changed** — decisions, direction, and anything that breaks.
- **What's next** — where the effort is going in the coming weeks.
- **From the community** — questions, contributions, and discussions
  worth surfacing.

## Where things live

- The project itself: [eigenius.io](https://eigenius.io) and
  [github.com/eigenius/eigenius](https://github.com/eigenius/eigenius).
- This community site: the newsletter archive, longer-form articles,
  and blog posts, all readable without an email address.
- Every issue is archived here the day it goes out.

## Subscribing

For now, the archive and its RSS feed *are* the subscription — email
delivery is planned, and when it arrives, existing readers won't need
to do anything. Point your feed reader at the newsletter feed to get
each issue as it's published.

See you next week.
