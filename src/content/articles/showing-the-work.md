AI agents are drafting more and more of the scientific and
engineering record, and the accountability surface science was built
on is binary: either you trust the author, or you don't use the
result. At human authoring speed that trade-off was tractable. At LLM
authoring speed it is not.

Eigenius answers structurally. Every claim lands in one of four
warrant categories — Observed, Derived, Verified, or Declared — and
the chain records *how* each claim is justified, so the reasoning
behind a conclusion is examinable rather than implicit in prose.

This article will walk through what that means in practice, from a
single measured value to a published designation.
